Run summary

Standing of these numbers. The transport half is verified physics: the solvers are checked against exact analytical solutions and conserve mass. The Adhesion Retention Index is a phenomenological model with parameters you supply. Its functional forms encode accepted degradation mechanisms and are bounded and monotone, but absolute ARI values are not validated against joint-strength measurements. Use ARI to rank designs and exposure conditions, not to predict residual bond strength.
