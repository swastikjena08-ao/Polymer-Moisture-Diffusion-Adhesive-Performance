Polymer Moisture Diffusion & Adhesive Performance

Computational study of moisture transport in polymer adhesive films
Research question How do polymer-film thickness, temperature, and water diffusivity influence the time-dependent loss of adhesive performance? Moisture arrival at the far face of the film is used as a proxy for potential performance change — this model predicts moisture transport, not measured bond strength.

6Diffusivity study

How water diffusivity affects penetration. The swept quantity is D_ref, the value quoted at T_ref — what a datasheet gives and what you set above. 9Model assumptions

  • One-dimensional diffusion through the film thickness only.
  • Fickian diffusion, with a single diffusion coefficient.
  • Constant diffusivity at a given temperature — no dependence on moisture concentration.
  • Arrhenius temperature dependence of diffusivity.
  • Simplified boundary conditions: the wetted face is held at equilibrium with the environment from t = 0; the far face is impermeable (one-sided) or a symmetry plane (two-sided).
  • Equilibrium uptake follows Henry's law — linear in relative humidity.
  • Uniform, dry initial condition.
  • No hydrolysis model.
  • No plasticization model.
  • No glass-transition model.
  • No swelling and no mechanical response.
  • No experimentally measured adhesion-strength prediction.
  • The moisture threshold is a proxy for potential performance change only.
